Nagu Soohir / Isdiwaan-geli

Qaybaha Koorsada & Tababarrada

Tababarka Farsamada & Disiplinta

Barashada maareynta, disiblinta gaarka ah, iyo sida loo wajaho hawlgallada iyo xaaladaha kala duwan.

Maamulka & Iskaashiga Rasmiga Ah

Garab-isstaagga iyo iskaashiga aan la leenahay hay'adaha amniga iyo madaxda tababarrada.

Xaqiijinta Nidaamka & Casharrada

Casharro aragti iyo shaqo oo isugu jira maamulka, nadaafadda, iyo ilaalinta shuruucda tababarka.

Maxay Ardaydu/Kaqeybgalayaashu Helayaan?

  • Cunto & Nafaqo: Cuntooyinka maalinlahay ah oo isu jifa nafaqo buuxda inta tababarku socdo.
  • Agab & Uniform: Dharka tababarka rasmiga ah (Taktiko/Militari) iyo kabaha gaarka ah.
  • Shahaado Caddeyn Ah: Shahaado xirfadeed oo ka socota ALIF K9 ACADEMY.
  • Aqoonta K9: Casharro ku saabsan maareynta, daawaynta, iyo baridda eeyaha ciidanka.
